Add a card

Applied on the server to every take the player records. Allowed: volume, highpass, lowpass, atempo, vibrato, tremolo, acrusher, aecho (same rules as the Custom voice effect). The server rejects anything else.

Format help

Text: one performance card per line, Title | Text. A tab works instead of the bar, so you can paste from a spreadsheet. Blank lines and lines starting with # are skipped.

JSON: an array, or a deck export ({"name": "…", "cards": […]}). Each card can have title, text, kind (performance, effect, constraint), weight (1–20), enabled, and for effect cards effect: {"chain": "-af …"}, for constraint cards constraint: {"blind": {"caption": true}, "noReference": true, "takeLimit": 1}.

Up to 200 cards per import. Nothing is saved until you press Import.
